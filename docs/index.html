Privacy Policy 🛡️

Data Collection

Kopniak does not collect any personal data. The application operates entirely on your computer and does not transmit any information to external servers or services.

What We Know About You

Kopniak stores configuration data locally on your Mac using macOS system preferences. This data includes your customization choices and preferences needed for the app to function properly.

All this data stays on your computer. It is never sent to us, Apple, or any third party.

Privacy Manifest

Kopniak includes a complete Apple Privacy Manifest declaration detailing all APIs and their purpose. You can review this in the App Store listing.

No Tracking

Kopniak does not:

  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Collect usage data
  • Access your files or documents
  • Access the internet
  • Track your activity or location

Your Privacy Matters

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. Kopniak is designed as a "zero-knowledge" application—what happens on your Mac stays on your Mac.
