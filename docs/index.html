Kopniak ∙ The Simplest Breaks

The most straightforward macOS app that reminds you to take regular breaks from your Mac.

Simple, private, and open-source.

Download from Mac App Store

Idea

In Ukrainian, "копняк" [kopˈnʲiak] means "a kick". The app gives you a regular kick so you don't forget to take a break from the computer. Kopniak is made by an indie developer (me) who uses it every day.

Features

  • Straightforward accessible design
  • Customizable break and snooze intervals
  • Optional menu bar icon with a countdown timer
  • Automatically resets or pauses the timer when the computer is locked
  • Simple and effective break reminders with optional sounds
  • Option to launch at login
  • Supports macOS 15+
  • Free and open-source
  • Share your feature requests and ideas on GitHub

Privacy

Kopniak does not collect any data. Everything stays on your computer.

The app does not:

  • Access the internet
  • Use analytics or tracking
  • Collect usage data
  • Access your files

Your settings are stored locally using macOS preferences and never leave your device.
